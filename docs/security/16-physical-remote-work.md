# Physical and Remote Work Security

| Version | Owner | Approved (sign and date) | Next review |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1.0 draft, 13 Nov 2026 | R. Vishnupriyen, founder | ______________________ | Nov 2027 |

- Laptops: full-disk encryption on (BitLocker / FileVault), screen lock after 5 minutes, automatic updates.
- Work in private: no customer screens visible in public places; lock the screen when away.
- No customer data on personal USB drives or personal cloud storage.
- The backup private key's paper copy is kept in a locked safe; the password-manager copy is protected by 2-factor.
- Servers are in the supplier's data centre (Hostinger); their physical controls are covered by their certificates (policy 13).
