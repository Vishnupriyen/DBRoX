## Commitment
Hanubix keeps distributors' and companies' business data safe, correct and available. The founder is responsible for
information security and reviews this policy every year and after any serious incident.

## Scope
The Hanubix server (Hostinger VPS), its database and backups, the Android app and web app, the control panel, the code on
GitHub, and everyone who works on Hanubix (the founder, staff, contractors and the AI team).

## Rules everyone follows
1. Use only your own login. Never share a PIN, key or password.
2. Keep secrets out of the code, chats and documents (they live only in `deploy/.env` on the server and the founder's offline copy).
3. Every change passes the quality gate and the independent tester before it goes live (policy 10).
4. Report anything that looks wrong at once to the founder (policy 9).
5. Personal data is collected only for a stated purpose and kept only as long as needed (policies 17 and 18).

## Roles
- **Founder**: owns this policy, approves risks and changes, holds the backup private key, runs the monthly access review.
- **Security & Operations (AI team)**: keeps the controls working, watches alerts, signs off releases.
- **Everyone**: follows the rules above.
