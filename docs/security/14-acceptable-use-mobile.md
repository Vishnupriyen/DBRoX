## Hanubix's own team
- Use work accounts only for Hanubix work; 2-factor login everywhere; a screen lock on every device.
- Never copy customer data to personal apps, chats or USB drives; never paste secrets into chats or AI tools.

## Distributor staff using the Hanubix app (shown to owners at onboarding)
- Keep a screen lock on the phone; do not share your PIN.
- The app locks after 5 minutes away, blocks screenshots, and needs an online login after 14 days without signal.
- **Lost phone**: tell the owner at once. The owner taps **Staff & access → Lock phone** (and Remove access if the person
  left). The phone's copy of the firm's data is removed at its next contact, or after 30 days without contact.
